## What I build

- **[MarkIt](https://mark-it.co)** is where everything you save, from WhatsApp, Telegram, Chrome or your phone's share sheet, lands in one library that sorts, tags and searches by meaning. Designed, built and shipped alone.
- **[markit-mcp](https://github.com/FuzulsFriend/markit-mcp)** connects Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other AI apps to that library over the Model Context Protocol. It is listed in the [official MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io/v0/servers?search=FuzulsFriend).
- **Claude Code skills**, free and open source. Each one came out of a problem I actually had.

## Training and consulting

- **For:** CEOs and leadership, CTOs and engineering teams, marketing and content teams, sales and RevOps, small business owners, and lawyers, accountants and advisors.
- **Topics:** Claude Code and agents, MCP and Skills, n8n automation, RAG, evals, token economics, AI in CRM, AI with privacy and regulation, prompt engineering.

A short intro call is usually enough to tell whether there is a fit.
